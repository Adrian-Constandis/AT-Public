# Taper Week Before Austria Camp

This week is a deliberate deload/taper from the coach's full plan — the goal is to arrive in Austria on Aug 2 fresh and healthy, not fatigued. Total volume is ~3–4 hours instead of ~9–10.5 hours, with only one shortened quality session.

---

## Monday – Easy Spin (No Intervals)

**Goal:** Loosen the legs, zero intensity, start unloading fatigue from last week's big volume spike.

| Time | Activity |
|---|---|
| 07:00 | Wake up, drink a glass of water |
| 07:15 | Breakfast: oatmeal or toast, eggs, fruit, milk |
| 08:00 – 09:00 | Free time, light stretching/mobility |
| 09:00 – 10:15 | **Easy ride, 60–75 min, 90–110W, no targets.** Focus on smooth pedaling, relaxed cadence drills (90–100 rpm), stay conversational-pace the whole time. No hard efforts, no sprints. |
| 10:15 | Recovery snack: milk + banana, or yogurt + fruit |
| 10:30 | Shower |
| 10:45 – 12:30 | Free time |
| 12:30 | Lunch: carbs (pasta/rice) + protein (chicken/fish/eggs/legumes) + vegetables |
| 13:30 – 15:00 | Quiet time / rest (reading, screen time, hobbies) |
| 15:00 | Snack: fruit + yogurt or a handful of nuts |
| 15:30 – 17:00 | Free time / hobbies |
| 17:00 – 17:15 | Light mobility or stretching (10 min) |
| 18:30 | Dinner: extra carbs, protein, vegetables, drink water steadily through the meal |
| 19:30 – 20:30 | Family time, relaxed activities |
| 20:30 | Wind-down: no screens, shower/bath if not done earlier |
| 21:00 | Bedtime routine (brush teeth, dim lights) |
| 21:15 | Lights out — target **9.5–10 hours of sleep** |

**Notes:** No power zones today. If he feels good, he can push the pace slightly on short rollers, but no sustained hard efforts. This is about movement and recovery, not fitness building.
